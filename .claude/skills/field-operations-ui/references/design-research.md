# Design research behind this skill

Reviewed 4 September 2026. These sources inform the workflow; their general advice does not override the user's product constraints.

- [OpenAI Product Design — Ideate](https://github.com/openai/role-specific-plugins/blob/main/plugins/product-design/skills/ideate/SKILL.md): use spacing, grouping, alignment, and typography before borders or shadows; avoid nested/repeated card treatment; preserve the supplied information architecture and choose realistic target dimensions.
- [OpenAI Product Design — Audit](https://github.com/openai/role-specific-plugins/blob/main/plugins/product-design/skills/audit/SKILL.md): audit the real flow with fresh screenshots, inspect every capture, tie findings to evidence, and state what screenshots cannot prove.
- [OpenAI Frontend App Builder](https://github.com/openai/plugins/blob/main/plugins/build-web-apps/skills/frontend-app-builder/SKILL.md): design the complete surface, extract a small coherent system, use proper vector icons for controls, and verify the browser result instead of stopping at source code.
- [Anthropic Frontend Design](https://github.com/anthropics/skills/blob/main/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md): ground the visual language in the subject and reject recognizable generated-design defaults such as identical rounded cards, gratuitous eyebrow labels, gradient washes, and generic template chrome.

Applied here: CVC restraint comes from sailing-course notation, days, boats, crews, the provided marks, and a functional blue/orange palette. The interface should remain calm and plain; distinctiveness must not become decoration.
