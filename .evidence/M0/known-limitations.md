# M0 known limitations

- The shell is deliberately non-functional beyond persistence diagnostics;
  course setup begins in M1.
- PowerSync multi-tab support was unavailable in the pinned browser test
  context; multi-tab and WebKit compatibility remain for their planned gates.
- OCR and STT decisions are feasibility baselines. Their product integrations,
  field-level confidence/review, mobile performance, and adverse-input testing
  belong to M4 and the later assisted-input work.
- PWA install icons and final CVC brand assets are not yet present; M1 owns the
  user-visible application shell.
